These images come from other projects and keep their own licenses; the
Unlicense of apps-site does not apply to them. When their apps are
visible, the generated site publishes them under assets/media/ with
content-hashed names. The originals are in catalogue/assets/ of
https://github.com/L-K-M/apps-site.
